# Relevant Works for Coalescence Manuscript

This document compiles academic works relevant to the manuscript on community coalescence, community-level selection, and interaction strength. Works are ordered by relevance/importance within each category.

---

## Category 1: Community Coalescence (Core Literature)

### Highest Priority - Directly Related

1. **Diaz-Colunga et al. (2022)** - "Top-down and bottom-up cohesiveness in microbial community coalescence"
   - **PNAS** | [Link](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2111261119)
   - Over 100 coalescence experiments showing ecological coselection where dominant taxa recruit rarer partners (top-down) and vice versa (bottom-up). Uses consumer-resource model. **Directly cited in your manuscript.**

2. **Rillig et al. (2015)** - "Interchange of entire communities: microbial community coalescence"
   - **Trends in Ecology & Evolution** | Foundational paper defining community coalescence concept. **Already cited.**

3. **Lechon et al. (2021)** - "Community coalescence: an eco-evolutionary perspective"
   - **Philosophical Transactions B** | [Link](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rstb.2019.0252)
   - Comprehensive review linking coalescence to eco-evolutionary dynamics.

4. **Liu et al. (2024)** - "Drivers and consequences of microbial community coalescence"
   - **ISME Journal** | [Link](https://academic.oup.com/ismej/article/18/1/wrae179/7759390)
   - Recent comprehensive review on coalescence drivers and outcomes.

5. **Castledine et al. (2020)** - "Community coalescence: an eco-evolutionary perspective"
   - Reviews coalescence from evolutionary perspective.

6. **Rillig et al. (2017)** - Additional coalescence framework paper.

7. **Lu et al. (2025)** - "Interplay of ecological processes modulates microbial community reassembly following coalescence"
   - **ISME Journal** | [Link](https://academic.oup.com/ismej/article/19/1/wraf041/8104870)
   - Recent work on ecological processes during coalescence reassembly.

8. **Tikhonov (2016)** - "Resource Competition: When communities collide"
   - **eLife** | [Link](https://elifesciences.org/articles/18753)
   - Theoretical framework for coalescence through resource competition lens. **Critical for your theory section.**

9. **Sierocinski et al. (2017)** - "Community coalescence stirs things up"
   - **Current Biology** | [Link](https://www.cell.com/current-biology/fulltext/S0960-9822(17)31330-1)
   - Perspective on coalescence implications.

## Category 3: Consumer-Resource Theory & Lotka-Volterra Models

### High Priority - Theoretical Foundation

14. **Tikhonov & Monasson (2017)** - "Statistical physics of community ecology: a cavity solution to MacArthur's consumer resource model"
    - **PMC** | [Link](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6329381/)
    - Statistical physics approach to consumer-resource models.

15. **Goldford et al. (2018)** - "Emergent simplicity in microbial community assembly"
    - **Science** | [Link](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aat1168)
    - Shows emergent simplicity despite complexity; metabolic by-product cross-feeding enables coexistence.

16. **Marsland et al. (2023)** - "Resource competition can explain simplicity in microbial community assembly"
    - **PNAS** | [Link](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2212113120)
    - Pairwise competition predicts multispecies outcomes via resource competition.

17. **Momeni et al. (2017)** - "Lotka-Volterra pairwise modeling fails to capture diverse pairwise microbial interactions"
    - **eLife** | [Link](https://elifesciences.org/articles/25051)
    - Important limitations of LV models for microbial systems.

18. **Clark et al. (2023)** - "When does a Lotka-Volterra model represent microbial interactions?"
    - **mSystems** | [Link](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/msystems.00757-22)
    - LV works well in complex, low-nutrient environments.

19. **Joseph et al. (2020)** - "Structural identifiability of the generalized Lotka-Volterra model for microbiome studies"
    - **Royal Society Open Science** | [Link](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rsos.201378)
    - Parameter identifiability in gLV models.

## Category 4: Interaction Strength & Nutrient Effects

### High Priority - Directly Relevant to Your Experimental Design

20. **Ratzke et al. (2020)** - "Strength of species interactions determines biodiversity and stability in microbial communities"

## Category 15: Competition vs. Cooperation Trade-offs

### Medium Priority

59. **Coyte et al. (2021)** - "Polarization of microbial communities between competitive and cooperative metabolism"
    - **Nature Ecology & Evolution** | [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-020-01353-4)
    - Two community types: cooperative (smaller genomes, auxotrophies) vs. competitive (larger genomes, antimicrobials). Trade-off in resilience.

60. **Weiland-Bräuer et al. (2021)** - "The role of competition versus cooperation in microbial community coalescence"
    - **PLOS Computational Biology** | [Link](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1009584)
    - **HIGHLY RELEVANT**: Competitive interactions preclude survival in coalescence; cooperation enhances coalescence success.

61. **Ghoul & Mitri (2016)** - "The Ecology and Evolution of Microbial Competition"
    - **Trends in Microbiology** | Classic review of competition mechanisms.
