\subsection*{B. 16S Sequencing Quality Control}
\label{sec:suppl:sequencing_qc}

All samples achieved $>$10,000 reads per sample after quality filtering. Technical replicates showed high reproducibility with Pearson correlation $>$0.95 between replicate abundance profiles. Samples with fewer than 1,000 reads after quality filtering were excluded from analysis.

Raw ASV counts were converted to relative abundances by total-sum normalization. ASVs below 0.1\% relative abundance in all samples were filtered out, corresponding to the extinction threshold used in simulations. For the similarity analysis, relative abundance profiles were further normalized to unit length.
