\subsection*{Supplementary Note 4: Nutrient perturbation and pairwise invasion resistance}
\label{sec:suppl:note4}

To directly measure pairwise interactions between species, we performed reciprocal invasion assays using the 12 most abundant isolates (ranked by ASV counts across all communities), testing each pair in both directions to assess competitive outcomes and detect potential bistability. Full assay protocol (95:5 resident:invader ratio, 7-cycle propagation, colony counting, and classification thresholds) is described in Supplementary Methods (Pairwise Invasion Assays section).

Invasion assays were performed across all three nutrient conditions: Nutr$-$, Base, and Nutr$+$. The frequency of competitive exclusion increased with nutrient concentration, consistent with the coalescence outcome patterns observed in community experiments. Under Nutr$-$, more pairs achieved stable coexistence, while Nutr$+$ conditions led to more frequent exclusion of one species by the other.
