\subsection*{B. 16S Sequencing and Data Processing}
\label{sec:suppl:sequencing}

Full details of the 16S rRNA sequencing and bioinformatic analysis pipeline are provided in the main text Methods section. Here we provide supplementary information on quality control and reproducibility.

% --------------------------------------------
% B.1 Sequencing Quality Metrics
% --------------------------------------------
\subsubsection*{B.1 Sequencing Quality Metrics}

All samples achieved $>$10,000 reads per sample after quality filtering. Technical replicates showed high reproducibility with Pearson correlation $>$0.95 between replicate abundance profiles. Samples with fewer than 1,000 reads after quality filtering were excluded from analysis.

% --------------------------------------------
% B.2 ASV Filtering and Normalization
% --------------------------------------------
\subsubsection*{B.2 ASV Filtering and Normalization}

Raw ASV counts were converted to relative abundances by total-sum normalization. ASVs below 0.1\% relative abundance in all samples were filtered out, corresponding to the extinction threshold ($10^{-3}$) used in simulations. For the vector decomposition analysis, relative abundance profiles were further L$_2$-normalized to unit vectors.
