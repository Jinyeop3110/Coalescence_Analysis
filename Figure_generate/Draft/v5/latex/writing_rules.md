# Writing Rules for Coalescence Manuscript

## Terminology Standards

### 1. Outcome Classification
- Use **"Dominance"** as the outcome category name for cases where one parental community wins
- Do not use **"CLS"** as an outcome category name
- Three outcomes: **Dominance**, **Mixture**, **Restructuring**
- Use **"Mixture"** for the outcome class, not "Mixing"
- When interpreting Dominance as evidence for community-level selection, distinguish the observed outcome category from the broader conceptual interpretation

### 2. Media Naming
- Use **"Base medium"** (capital B, lowercase m)
- Use **"Nutr−"** and **"Nutr+"** for nutrient conditions
- Plural: "media" (not "mediums")

### 3. Hyphenation
- Always hyphenate: **"community-level selection"**
- Always hyphenate: **"top-down regime"**

### 4. Parent Communities
- Use **"parental communities"** (not "parent communities")
- Consistent: "parental community A", "parental community B"
- Do not use "parent" or "parents" as standalone nouns in author-written **prose or captions** when referring to the two communities being coalesced; use "parental community" or "parental communities"
- **Exception, figure labels (author decision, 2026-08-01).** Axis tick labels, axis titles and legend entries inside figure artwork may use "parent"/"parents" as standalone nouns. Space inside a panel is tight, and the full term forces a line wrap that shrinks the label below the 5 pt floor. The canonical short forms are **"Same parent"** and **"Cross parents"**, in sentence case.
- When a caption quotes a figure label, it must quote the label **as drawn**. Do not silently expand "Same parent" to "Same parental community" in the caption: the reader is being pointed at text they can see in the figure.

### 5. Regime Naming
- Two regimes: **"top-down regime"** and **"emergent regime"**
- Do NOT use: "species-driven regime", "Strong as Species", "Strong as Communities"

### 6. Experimental Parameters
- Dilution factor: **×30 every 24 h** (not 1:100)
- Stabilization: 7 days of serial dilution

### 7. Other Standards
- Interaction strength symbol: μ (mu)
- Use "post-coalescence community" or "coalesced community" consistently
- Spell out "community-level selection" unless the abbreviation "CLS" is explicitly defined in the same text unit, such as a figure caption or section
- Avoid "CLS" in figure labels unless the caption defines it
- Mark second-round manuscript and supplementary edits with `\revsecond{...}` so they render blue in `main.pdf` and `supplementary.pdf`
- These terminology standards also apply to response-letter captions, response-only figure labels, response-figure generation code, and figure provenance text
